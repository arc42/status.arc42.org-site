{%- comment -%} Site footer — the arc42 family footer. LOCAL OVERRIDE of the vendored minimal-mistakes 4.24.0 include of the same name. The theme's version renders site.author's social profiles as a row of FontAwesome glyphs; this one is the family footer, ported from docs.arc42.org-site/_includes/site-footer.html and cross-checked against arc42.org-site/_includes/footer.html. Adapted for this site: docs' Contact link and its own /imprint/ point at arc42.org instead, which is where this site's imprint lives — status.arc42.org is a dashboard, not a content site, and has no pages of its own to link to. Its Status link is dropped, because this *is* status. Two things the previous footer carried are kept deliberately: · the CC BY-SA 4.0 statement (README.md; the licence of the arc42 material this site reports on), and · the INNOQ support credit — now on the family's monochrome asset, not the retired petrol/apricot one (BRAND.md, "Sponsor mark": both of its hexes are on the CI deny-list). No icons. At footer size the labels carry all the meaning and the glyphs only added noise — the same call the whole family made on 2026-07-30, and part of why FontAwesome could leave this site entirely. Styles: _sass/_arc42-family.scss (.supported-by-innoq is declared in assets/css/arc42-status-style.css and is not re-declared there). The build line names which release of THIS SITE you are looking at: site.version from _config.yml (bumped by hand), plus the commit and deploy date from _data/build.yml, which .github/workflows/pages.yml writes at deploy time and .gitignore keeps out of the repository. A local build has no build.yml, so it prints the version alone rather than claiming a deploy that never happened. The statistics service's own version is a different number and stays in its table footer. {%- endcomment -%}
